## Workflow

Identify the current step from its heading or selected progress label. Tenants can omit, rename or reorder steps. Previously observed tenant steps are hints. Read the visible controls on an unfamiliar screen. Use the existing native actions and request an image only when the text leaves a control ambiguous.

After Save and Continue, observe the page. A new current-step heading verifies advancement. If the same step remains, check the top and inline errors, follow the error to its named field, correct it, then check again. Wait for a pending transition before trying another action. Resume the existing draft after an interruption.

## Start

On a fresh application choose Autofill with Resume when offered and upload the approved original once. Check the filename and wait for the parsed sections to appear. On a returning application continue the draft. Reimporting can overwrite edits.

## Account

Use the employer account action for this tenant. Distinguish sign-in, account creation and verification from application submission. Continue the same requisition after sign-in.

## My Information

Compare contact information, legal and preferred names, address, phone type and referral source with confirmed facts. Verify the selected country and any dependent address fields after they refresh.

## My Experience

Audit parsed employment and education against the profile, including dates and current-role checkboxes. Inspect existing blocks before adding a missing entry. Remove only a verified empty or exact duplicate draft block. Read the affected block again before editing another.

For a searchable school, major or skill, open its field and enter a short query. Use its visible search action and wait for the options. Choose a supported match, verify the selected tag or value, then leave the widget. Typed search text alone does not establish a selection. Enter month and year in the labeled segments and reread both. Verify the original résumé is attached.

## Application Questions

Use the exact wording of each question. Distinguish professional years, academic experience, work authorization, future sponsorship and clearance. Use confirmed answers for the employer and role. Complete independently answerable fields before flagging a required unknown.

## Voluntary Disclosures

Match each optional question to its own saved preference. Race and Hispanic or Latino ethnicity are separate questions. Check the selected answer after leaving each menu.

## Self Identify

Read which disclosure the form requests. Use confirmed choices. Check any required name and date. Accept required employer terms on the form and continue without opening linked notices.

## Review

Check the employer, requisition, résumé filename, parsed history, eligibility answers and disclosures. Use the existing final review and submission action once. Count success only after the employer shows a receipt for this application. If the outcome is uncertain, retain it for reconciliation.
